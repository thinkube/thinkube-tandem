= The space — asks, changes, units
:page-type: concept

The space is the one surface between you and the machine. It renders in
the sidebar (the Tandem icon), where every repository's thinking spaces
are listed, and each thinking space you open (*Tandem: Open the Space*)
gets its own editor tab, titled with that space's name. Opening one space
leaves every other space's tab exactly as it was — several can be open at
once, each showing its own live state.

== What derivation does with an ask

Behind one captured ask runs a pipeline of rounds, each fail-soft:

. *Contextualize* — one bounded, cited reading of the repository,
  persisted per ask, so later rounds build on an established reading.
. *Ground* — the ask becomes changes: sentences with real landing files
  and acceptance criteria. Line numbers are refused by design — anchors
  are structural (file, symbol), so they survive edits.
. *Gap-close* — a judge with exactly two outcomes: the set covers the
  ask, or here are the missing changes, concrete and grounded.
. *Impact* — the adjacent code that must move too (callers,
  configuration, documentation).
. *Intent coverage* — every clause of your ask must be served by some
  change; an unserved clause comes back as a question with a
  recommendation, never silence.
. *Assessment* — vague acceptance criteria are rewritten to observable
  ones (closed verdicts: observable / vague / untestable).
. *Challenger* — contradictions between the changes and your decisions
  in force are raised as questions. It never changes anything.

== Units and pins

Changes cluster into units by real code coupling (same file is decisive).
The clustering is code, not a model — and your pins outrank it:
*Merge into one slice* forces a union; *Split out* severs a pair.

== Staleness and re-grounding

Every grounded change carries a fingerprint of the repository state it
was true for. When the code moves underneath, the change is marked stale;
press the stale badge to re-ground. A signed TEP whose content drifted
refuses dispatch until re-signed.

== Panic

The *Panic* button (behind a confirm) clears everything derived — changes,
units, open questions, unsigned cuts — and keeps what is yours: your asks
and your settled decisions. It is refused once any TEP was signed in the
space.
