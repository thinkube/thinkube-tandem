= What Tandem is
:page-type: concept

Tandem is pair development for a team of two — one human, one AI — over a
git repository. You say what you want in your own words; the machine
grounds it in the real code; you sign exactly what you saw; workers build
it under verification you never have to take on faith; you accept the
delivery by reading its proofs.

== The shape

Everything moves through one surface (the space) and two decisions that
are always yours:

. *Sign* — freezing a cut of grounded changes into a TEP. Nothing is
  built before this.
. *Accept* — merging a delivery whose proofs are green. Nothing lands
  after the build without this.

Between the gates the machine is autonomous, and it is not trusted: probes
are written by workers that cannot see the implementation, completion is
decided by a black-box oracle rather than a worker's claim, files outside
a worker's declared footprint are reverted, and everything that could not
be delivered is written on the delivery's face.

== The vocabulary

ask:: Your words, kept verbatim. Everything derives from asks; asks are
never machine-edited.
change:: One grounded intention — a sentence, the file(s) it lands in,
and the acceptance criteria that prove it.
unit:: Changes clustered by code coupling; the thing you add to a cut.
cut:: The set of changes you are about to freeze.
TEP:: A signed cut — named `TEP-<you>-<n>`, bound by a cryptographic
token to exactly what you saw when you signed.
slice:: The engine's dispatch unit inside a TEP: one coder, its held-out
probe authors, a footprint.
delivery:: A branch plus its proofs, offered for your accept.
finding:: Something the machine noticed, could not settle, and has no
actor left to route to. It rides the delivery for you to weigh; it never
blocks the accept.
observation:: An acceptance criterion only the running product can show.
Carried for you to confirm by using the thing, never graded green by the
machine.
project:: One repository, or one subtree of it, with a minted identity
that your spaces hang from — see xref:identity.adoc[].
